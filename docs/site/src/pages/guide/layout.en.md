The **Layout** page shows every screen of every member on one canvas, the same on every device.

## Place them {#place}

Drag each device where its screens are on your desk: the borders snap to the others. Past a border that touches another screen, your cursor goes on to that device, with your keyboard: moves, clicks, wheel and keys go where the cursor is. A device offline is a wall.

> [!TIP]
> <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd>, or **Bring my cursor back** (panel, tray), brings the cursor home at once.

## The live map {#map}

The map shows where the cursor of every member is, live. Drag the background to move the view, use the wheel to zoom, **Recenter** in the corner to see everything again; the view never moves by itself.

## Profiles {#profiles}

A **profile** saves the layout under a name (desk, travel...): apply it on every member at once, delete it when it is no longer needed.

## Show where you are {#presence}

| Action | Effect |
| ------ | ------ |
| **Look here** | A wave where your cursor is, on its screen and on every map |
| **Laser** | A red trail on the screens your cursor crosses |
| **Follow** | An arrow at the border of your screen points at the cursor of a member |
